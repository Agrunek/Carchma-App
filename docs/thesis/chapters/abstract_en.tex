The engineering thesis entitled ``Web application as a car sale advertisement service'' focuses on the development of an online platform for publishing advertisements for passenger car sales.
The system implements mechanisms aimed at improving customer protection against fraudulent offers, such as allowing users to post reviews under advertisements, a seller reputation system influencing the positioning of offers in search results, publicly accessible advertisement archives, as well as options for reporting and moderating content.

The architecture is based on a server-side component and a client application that communicate over the network using requests adapted to a role and permission based model.
The reputation system relies on an algorithm that processes user reviews associated with past offers and subsequently utilizes the calculated value within the search engine.

The development of the platform involved the use of numerous modern and proven technologies, including \emph{React}, \emph{Zod}, \emph{MongoDB}, and \emph{TanStack Router}.
Particular emphasis was placed on communication stability, proper error handling, and thorough validation of data submitted via forms.
The completed project is highly scalable and consistent with best practices for developing such solutions.
